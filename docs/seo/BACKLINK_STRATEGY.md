# RedlineD1 — Backlink Strategy

Last updated: 2026-07-21

---

## Status

**Stage D (Backlink CRM and automated outreach) is explicitly deferred.** This document records the strategy framework for when Stage D begins. Do not implement any automated outreach system until Stage D is explicitly initiated.

---

## Target link categories

### Tier 1 — High authority, direct relevance

| Category | Examples | Notes |
|----------|---------|-------|
| Auto repair industry publications | Motor Age, AutoInc, ShopOwner Magazine | Editorial coverage, not paid |
| Trade association websites | ASA, NAPA, AAA supplier resources | Resource page links |
| Independent mechanic communities | Reddit r/MechanicAdvice, iATN, AutoServiceWorld forums | Organic mentions |

### Tier 2 — Tool/resource links

| Category | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| "Best free auto repair tools" roundups | Target with the labor rate calculator and DVI checklist |
| Mechanic education blogs | Link to DVI checklist and repair order template |
| Small business software directories | G2, Capterra, Software Advice — listing pages |

### Tier 3 — Community and reference

| Category | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| YouTube mechanics who mention software | Organic mentions from tool users |
| Local business directories | NAP consistency (Name, Address, Phone) — not link-building focused |

## Outreach rules (for when Stage D begins)

- No mass outreach
- No purchased links
- No private blog networks (PBNs)
- No link exchanges
- Outreach only to genuinely relevant sites where the content adds value
- All outreach must be manually reviewed before sending
- Do not claim statistics that do not exist when pitching coverage

---

## NAP (Name/Address/Phone) consistency

If local SEO is pursued for D1 Imports locations, NAP data must be consistent across all directories:
- Business name: use the same format everywhere
- Do not create conflicting Google Business Profile listings for the same address
- D1 Imports location data is private business data — do not expose in public SEO content without explicit instruction

---

## Competitor comparison link strategy

Comparison pages (`/compare/redlined1-vs-tekmetric`, etc.) are the highest-probability pages for organic backlinks from:
- Forums where mechanics discuss software alternatives
- Reddit threads comparing shop management tools
- YouTube description links from review creators

No special action required — just ensuring these pages are public, indexed, and accurate (they are as of this audit).
