SI-13 Customer Lifetime Intelligence — Staff UAT

Branch: feature/si-13-customer-lifetime-intelligence  |  Date: 2026-07-10
All features OFF by default. Enable flags in Supabase Dashboard to test.

Setup

Enable in Supabase feature_flags:

  • customer_lifetime_intelligence → true
  • customer_intelligence_panel → true
  • customer_segmentation → true
  • customer_revenue_opportunities → true

Test Scenarios

Scenario 1 — Profile Build
  1. Navigate to any customer page.
  2. Confirm CustomerLifetimePanel is visible below main info.
  3. Confirm panel loads without blocking the page.

Pass: Panel shows financial summary, relationship score, retention risk.

Scenario 2 — Segment Classification
  1. Open a customer with 10+ visits and paid invoices.
  2. Check segments card.

Pass: Shows VIP or High Value with primary badge (★).

Fail condition: "Price Sensitive" label is visible to end user.

Scenario 3 — Retention Risk
  1. Find a customer with last visit > 12 months ago.
  2. Open their intelligence panel.

Pass: Shows High Risk or Critical Risk.

Pass: Suggested actions are informational only. No automatic actions performed.

Scenario 4 — Declined Work Opportunities
  1. Open a customer who previously declined work.
  2. Check Opportunities card.

Pass: Opportunity listed with disclaimer text visible.

Pass: Disclaimer includes "Requires technician inspection and advisor review."

Scenario 5 — Timeline
  1. Open any active customer with service history.
  2. Check Service History section.

Pass: Events sorted newest first. Includes jobs, estimates, invoices.

Scenario 6 — Financial Summary
  1. Open customer with paid and unpaid invoices.

Pass: Outstanding balance shown in red when > 0. Disclaimer visible.

Scenario 7 — Feature Flag OFF
  1. Disable customer_lifetime_intelligence flag.
  2. Reload customer page.

Pass: Panel not shown. Core customer info still visible. Page loads normally.

Scenario 8 — New Customer / No Data
  1. Open a newly created customer with no history.

Pass: Panel shows gracefully. No crash. Shows "Limited Data" segment.

Scenario 9 — Core Workflow Isolation
  1. Create an estimate, invoice, and job card.

Pass: None blocked or slowed. No automatic actions triggered.

Scenario 10 — Rebuild API
  1. POST to /api/intelligence/customer/{customerId}

Pass: Returns 200 with result. No SMS/email/invoice created automatically.
